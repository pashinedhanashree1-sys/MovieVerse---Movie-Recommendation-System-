How MovieVerse Works

This project uses K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) collaborative filtering on the MovieLens dataset to recommend similar movies based on user ratings. By measuring how closely your taste matches other viewers, the model finds hidden gems you're likely to enjoy next.
